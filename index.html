KINFX 速成輸入法 — 私隱政策

Privacy Policy · 生效日期 2026-10-26 · 版本 1.0

本輸入法不收集、不傳送、不儲存你輸入的任何文字。應用程式沒有申請互聯網權限（INTERNET），技術上無法把任何資料送出你的裝置。

一、我們不收集的資料

本程式不收集、不要求、不存取以下任何一項：

  • 你輸入的字、詞、句子或任何文字內容
  • 打字記錄、候選字選擇記錄、學習記錄
  • 位置、聯絡人、通話記錄、短訊、相片、麥克風、相機
  • 裝置識別碼、廣告識別碼（Advertising ID）、IMEI
  • 帳戶資料 — 本程式沒有帳戶系統、沒有登入

本程式不含廣告、不含分析或追蹤 SDK、不含社交平台登入、不含任何第三方數據收集元件。

二、剪貼簿

只有在你主動按下鍵盤上的「貼」鍵那一刻，程式會讀取一次剪貼簿內容，並插入目前文字欄位。

  • 不會在背景監聽或輪詢剪貼簿
  • 不會記錄或保存剪貼簿歷史
  • 不會上傳任何剪貼簿內容
  • 按「複製」時，只把你已選取的文字寫入剪貼簿，與系統其他複製操作相同

三、存放於你裝置內的資料

設定值聯想字開關、雙擊空白出句號開關。存於應用程式私有儲存空間，只有本程式可讀取。解除安裝即一併刪除。
雲端備份已關閉（allowBackup="false"）。設定不會同步到 Google 帳戶或其他雲端。
詞庫速成碼表、聯想詞、英漢對照表隨程式安裝，屬唯讀資料，不會因你打字而改變，亦不會上傳。

四、權限

本程式沒有申請任何 Android 權限。

作為輸入法，系統要求你自行在「設定 → 系統 → 語言與輸入」啟用本鍵盤，並可能顯示系統警告提示輸入法可讀取你輸入的內容。該提示由 Android 系統統一發出，適用於所有輸入法，並非本程式額外要求的權限。本程式即使收到該存取權，亦沒有任何網絡傳輸能力。

六、兒童

本程式並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，亦不收集任何資料，包括兒童的資料。

七、你的權利

由於本程式不收集亦不持有任何個人資料，並不存在需要查閱、更正、匯出或刪除的資料。解除安裝應用程式即可清除全部本機設定。你亦可隨時在系統設定停用或移除本鍵盤。

八、政策變更

如本政策有實質變更，會更新本頁並修改上方生效日期。若變更涉及開始收集資料，會在應用程式更新說明中另行通知。

Privacy Policy (English)

Effective 26 October 2026 · v1.0

KINFX Sucheng does not collect, transmit, or store anything you type. The app requests no INTERNET permission and is therefore technically incapable of sending data off your device.

What we do not collect

No keystrokes, no input history, no learning data, no location, contacts, messages, photos, microphone or camera access, no device or advertising identifiers, and no account data — the app has no accounts and no sign-in.

There are no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no social logins, and no third-party data collection components.

Clipboard

The clipboard is read once, only when you tap the Paste key, and inserted into the current text field. It is never monitored in the background, never logged, never stored as history, and never uploaded. Copying writes your selected text to the clipboard exactly as any system copy does.

Data stored on your device

Two preference switches (word association, double-space period) are stored in the app's private storage and are removed when you uninstall. Android auto-backup is disabled (allowBackup="false"), so preferences are not synced to any cloud. The bundled dictionaries are read-only and never uploaded.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions. Enabling a keyboard is done by you in system settings; the warning Android shows about input methods reading what you type is a system-wide notice that applies to every keyboard, and this app has no network capability regardless.

Third-party components

Sucheng code table derived from Rime (upstream LGPL-3.0; the original table descends from Chu Bong-Foo's public-domain work); association words from rime-cantonese (CC BY 4.0); English–Chinese gloss from CC-CEDICT (CC BY-SA 4.0); English word list from SCOWL (Kevin Atkinson, permissive).

Children

Not directed at children under 13. No data is collected from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or held, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete. Uninstalling the app clears all local preferences. You may disable or remove the keyboard in system settings at any time.

Changes

Material changes will be published on this page with a new effective date. If a change ever involves collecting data, it will also be disclosed in the app's update notes.
